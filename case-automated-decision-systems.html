Case study · Intelligent Payables Clearing

Autonomous decision systems for Accounts Payable

How Aesh streamlined accounts payable for a major U.S. water utility with OpenText’s AI-powered Vendor Invoice Management, replacing manual, multi-day reconciliation with touchless automation to support decision-making.

Industry
Utility — water services
Scale
1 billion gallons of water daily to 14 million people across 14 states
Workforce

Featured solutions
Vendor Invoice Management · Invoice Capture Center

Before · The challenge

Accounts payable teams spend hours manually extracting invoice data, cross-referencing purchase orders and goods receipts, and resolving discrepancies. This labor-intensive process delays cash flow and strains supplier relationships. To address these challenges, Aesh was hired to lead solution design, integration, and program governance for OpenText Vendor Invoice Management. The solution streamlines invoice processing from receipt to posting with minimal human intervention through AI-powered data capture, intelligent routing, and automated approval workflows.

Success criteria

  • Invoice exception resolution time2 hours 15 minutes
  • Manual effort per exception5 days 2 hours
  • 3-way match accuracy30% 85%

Product objectives

  • Automate PDF supplier invoice data extraction with high accuracy
  • Execute 3-way match (invoice vs PO vs GR) against live SAP data
  • Detect and classify invoice exceptions with root-cause analysis
  • Guide AP specialists toward appropriate corrective actions via human-in-the-loop interaction

The solution

Automated invoice management from receipt to posting

OpenText Vendor Invoice Management (VIM), integrated with SAP ERP, automates each stage of the invoice lifecycle. AI-powered OCR and document information extraction with Invoice Capture Center captures invoice data; intelligent routing and automated workflows handle approvals and exceptions; a Fiori app gives the team real-time visibility.

Next · Clean-core extension

Achieve a clean-core solution with an Agent Extension in Joule Studio 2.0

Extend the Receivables Payables Clearing Agent with Invoice Intelligence by adding PDF extraction, 3-way match, and exception guidance.

The solution adds new tools and reasoning steps to handle invoice-specific workflows, calling SAP S/4HANA Cloud OData APIs for PO and GR data retrieval.

  1. PDF Invoice Extraction

    AI-based extraction of key fields (invoice number, date, vendor, line items, amounts) from uploaded PDF supplier invoices.

  2. 3-Way Match Engine

    Automated comparison of extracted invoice data against Purchase Order (via CE_PURCHASEORDER_0001) and Goods Receipt (via SSPOPENITMGOODSRECEIPT_0001) data retrieved from SAP S/4HANA Cloud.

  3. Exception Detection & Analysis

    Identify discrepancies (price, quantity, missing GR, PO tolerance breaches), classify exception types, and provide root-cause analysis.

  4. Human-in-the-Loop Guidance

    Present actionable recommendations (e.g., contact supplier, request credit memo, escalate for approval) to AP specialists to resolve exceptions efficiently.
